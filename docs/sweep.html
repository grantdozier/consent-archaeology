WHAT'S ACTUALLY HAPPENING

We are fetching public web pages from a fixed, human-reviewed list of data-broker sites and reading what they publish about the identity you verified. That is the entire operation. It is a scraper in a trench coat.

Things this is not doing: tracing IP addresses, breaching anything, accessing private databases, or searching the open internet. Broker listing pages are public — that is the scandal — and we are reading yours, from a list anyone can audit in the source.

The progress bar is wired to actual page fetches reported by the server. It moves when a broker site finishes loading and at no other time. When it stalls, a data broker's website is being slow, which — considering what they charge for your address — feels about right.

Each broker also gets its corporate record pulled: legal entity name, headquarters address, registry ID. They collected your address history. It seemed only polite to collect theirs.

CONSENT ARCHAEOLOGY is a self-help letter generator, not a law firm. Nothing here is legal advice; every document is sent by you, as yourself, in your own name.
